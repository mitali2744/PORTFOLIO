Background

A bit about me and my academic journey.

I'm a third-year Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering student at PCCoE, Pune who's spent the last two years building full-stack web applications. My background in hardware gives me a different lens when designing backend systems and understanding constraints that most web developers overlook.
